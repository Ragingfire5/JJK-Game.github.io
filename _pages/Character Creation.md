== Character ==
In the Jujutsu world, your character has a lot of variety to choose from, ranging from robots who launch missiles to being a literal panda.


=== Class ===
In the Jujutsu Kaisen world there is a multiversal energy called cursed energy, it originates from human's negative emotions and can be used to exorcise evil spirits known as curses. Although in rare occasions, there's also the chance you can be born with little to no cursed energy, making you an anomaly by the world's standards. You can choose one of the following classes when creating your character:

== Jujutsu Sorcerer ==
Jujutsu sorcerers are the main fighters against the supernatural, protecting the world from cursed spirits by using cursed energy.

== [[Heavenly Restriction (Jujutsu Kaisen Supplement)|Heavenly Restriction]] ==
While some are blessed with innate talent for jujutsu, some have a body partially, or even fully restricted to cursed energy in exchange to being blessed in physical attributes.


=== Races ===
The world of Jujutsu Kaisen is mostly inhabitated by humans, but due to the negative energy people usually let leak out of their bodies the world has been filled with cursed spirits. You may choose between six of the following races:

== Human ==
This is the most predominant race in the Jujutsu world, you may choose to pick the Human variant to gain a [[Feats (Jujutsu Kaisen Supplement)|feat]] of your choice.

== Cursed Vessel ==
The Cursed Vessels are humans who have directly consumed cursed objects and now have gained cursed energy in exchange for having to share their body with a new host.

== Cursed Spirit ==
The Cursed Spirits are beings made entirely out of cursed energy, they are the main threat against humanity's safety due to killing anything in their path for power.

== Cursed Corpse ==
Cursed corpses are normally inanimate objects who were possessed as a curse, and are generally hostile towards humans. However, cursed corpses may also be created by sorcerers.

== Death Painting ==
Death paintings are like vessels, however they are different due to their origin.

== Simurian ==
Aliens from a far away planet, wielding cursed energy in their third eye, and techniques with otherworldly concepts, searching for a new home among the stars.



=== Backgrounds ===
Jujutsu Sorcerers have lots of stories, ranging from people who joined the world from eating fingers to others joining because they thought a woman was hot. 

You have the following backgrounds to choose from:

Sorcerer Student
Sorcerer Family
Reincarnated
New Shadow Style Student

Curse User
